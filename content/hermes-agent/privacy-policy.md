**Effective date: 21 September 2026**

Hermes Agent is a personal automation application operated by Slava Kuznetsov.

## Google user data

When you authorize Hermes Agent to access a Google account, it accesses only the Google services and data covered by the OAuth permissions you grant. Depending on those permissions, this can include Gmail messages and labels, Calendar events, Drive files, Contacts, Sheets, and Docs.

Hermes Agent uses this data only to provide the automation you request, including reading information, preparing drafts, and performing actions you explicitly authorize. It does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, profiling, or training general-purpose models. Google user data is not shared except with service providers necessary to provide the requested functionality, where required by law, or with your direction.

## Storage, retention, and security

Authorization credentials and operational records are stored only as needed to provide the service. Reasonable measures are used to protect them, but no system can guarantee absolute security. You can revoke Hermes Agent's Google access at any time in your [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## Contact and deletion requests

For privacy questions or to request deletion of stored application data, contact [hermes.smith3v@gmail.com](mailto:hermes.smith3v@gmail.com).
